coding this feature has genuinely been the highlight of my day today

it was easier than i thought to get the theme switcher working, and i didn't even need to use that much javascript to do it! (shoutout to the goat Kevin Powell's [tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fyuao3G-2qg); i really like his philosophy of uncomplicated CSS in general)

it's too bad that i can't rlly use <code>appearance: base-select;</code> to further customize the theme dropdown, as Firefox [currently does not support it](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/appearance#browser_compatibility); such is the price of being based, I suppose...

i've also added some custom fonts for this website, namely **DejaVu Sans Mono** for the body text and **Times New Roman MT Condensed** for headers. the choice of DejaVu Sans is specifically inspired by [the beguinage](beguine.net), whose web design influenced the directions i took for my own site. the Times New Roman, meanwhile, is influenced by uhhhh, [Evangelion?](https://graphicdesign.stackexchange.com/questions/45310/what-is-the-font-used-in-evangelions-intro-screen) i dont even watch the series myself, and yet its juicy title screen font wormed its way into my brain somehow. good typography just sticks with you like that
